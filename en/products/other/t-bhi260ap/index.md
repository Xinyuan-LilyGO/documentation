## Overview

The T-BHI260AP AI Smart Sensor is a multifunctional motion monitoring expansion module integrating a **self-learning AI algorithm** with a **6-axis IMU** (accelerometer + gyroscope). It supports real-time pedestrian tracking, personalized fitness data analysis (movement accuracy, calorie consumption), and specialized swimming metrics (stroke efficiency, stroke frequency). Features a waterproof design and low-power consumption for sustained outdoor and underwater use. Compatible with mainboards including T-Deck, T-Display-S3, T-Display-S3-Pro, and T-Echo.
