## Overview

T-Display K230 Cased Version is a high-performance AIoT terminal based on the K230 AI chip. It integrates a 4.1-inch AMOLED display, camera, audio, Ethernet, HDMI, USB, TF card, QWIIC, and expansion headers. The enclosure provides structural protection and interface cutouts, making it suitable for AI vision, edge computing, handheld demos, and peripheral expansion tests.

## Key Features

- K230 dual 64-bit RISC-V CPU, CPU1 1.6GHz + CPU0 800MHz
- Integrated KPU, DPU, GPU, and VPU for AI inference and multimedia processing
- 4.1-inch AMOLED display, 568 × 1232 resolution
- Onboard 2MP 1080P camera, microphone, speaker, and 3.5mm headphone jack
- Supports 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, BLE, RTL8152B 100 Mbps Ethernet, HDMI, USB, TF card, and QWIIC
- Enclosure size approx. 110 × 58 × 30mm
